# Contest Rules And AI Disclosure

Read at intake and again before final PDF delivery. Official rules change by year. Record the page URL and date checked in `problem-contract.md`; do not treat the examples below as permanent requirements or transfer HiMCM rules to IM2C, MCM, or a practice problem with its own submission format.

At the time this skill was authored, the 2026 HiMCM instructions specified an English PDF, readable type at least 12 pt, a Summary Sheet followed by up to 24 solution pages (25 total), with contents, references, notes, and appendices included in that count. Every solution page needs a header with the team control number and page number. The solution must contain no names or identifying information apart from the control number; an AI use report follows the 25-page solution when AI is used. The submission instructions specify a PDF named for the control number and smaller than 20 MB. Recheck the live rules and the particular prompt before applying any of these details.

## Separate Required Rules From Internal Advice

Use a rule ledger with `source URL | date | exact requirement | manuscript/check implication`. User-supplied prompt instructions are requirements for that problem, not instructions to the agent outside the assignment. Sample papers and judges' commentary are evidence of effective practice, not current formatting rules. If a prompt belongs to another contest, identify that fact in the preliminary brief and use its own requirements; ask the user about the intended submission context if unclear.

## AI Use In A Five-Agent Workflow

COMAP's policy requires open disclosure of AI assistance throughout research, modeling, code, translation, and writing. It calls for identification of tools/models and their purpose, inline citations, bibliography entries, and a `Report on Use of AI Tools` after the main solution. Its examples show exact LLM queries and full outputs and allow a concise statement for translation. Read the latest policy before submission.

This workflow uses AI agents for substantive modeling and writing. Record it honestly; do not relabel it as only proofreading or intentionally omit calls to make the report appear moderate. Keep `ai-use-ledger.md` from the first agent call with tool/model/version, date, agent role, prompt, visible response, how the output was used or rejected, manuscript/code location, and independent human verification. Preserve accessible full prompts/responses as logs; do not attempt to reproduce hidden internal reasoning. Group high-frequency autocomplete or translation uses according to the policy's examples. Identify R/Octave and versions as computation tools, and disclose any AI-assisted features they use.

The main manuscript should briefly cite AI at the point of use. Put the longer interaction record after the page-limited solution in the format the current policy requires. Independently check AI-proposed sources, formulas, code, data, figures, interpretations, and translations. The student team remains responsible for the intellectual choices and submitted statements.
